## SC-004: Microsoft Teams external sharing

**Risk:** Medium–High

**Situation:** An employee shares a confidential operational document with an external contractor through Teams.

| Element | Design |
|---|---|
| Channel | Teams / SharePoint |
| Detection | Confidential label |
| Action | Restrict inappropriate external sharing |
| Override | Approved external collaboration |
| Investigation | Review sharing activity and recipients |

**Suggested:** The business may depend on external logistics partners. The objective is to prevent unauthorised sharing, not eliminate legitimate collaboration.
